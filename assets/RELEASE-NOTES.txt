0.1.0-dev.1
===========

PetToys.BigDecimal.Core

- The N format specifier now honours the whole of the culture's group size list,
  as System.Decimal does. A culture that groups three digits and then two is
  grouped that way rather than uniformly in threes, a size list ending in zero
  stops grouping where it says to, and a culture that asks for no grouping gets
  none rather than groups of three. Output changes under those cultures.
- Formatting with N no longer allocates. It cost 64 bytes on every call, in the
  char and the UTF-8 overload alike, and was the last exception to the package's
  zero-allocation guarantee.
