Unreleased
----------
- BigDecimal.WithScale sets a value to a requested scale, padding with zeros as
  well as rounding, so a value can be presented at the scale a database column
  declares. Round remains narrowing-only.
- The representable range is documented and settled: a magnitude of 0..2^256-1
  with a scale of 0..255, so 77 significant digits always fit and the range
  runs from 1e-255 to roughly 1.157e77. Earlier text quoting 76 digits was
  wrong.
- Reading a power of ten no longer allocates, which makes the package claim of
  never allocating true again: addition, multiplication, division, rounding,
  parsing and WithScale all allocate nothing.
- The public surface carries XML documentation, and the build now fails on an
  undocumented public member.
